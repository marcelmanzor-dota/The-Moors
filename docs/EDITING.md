# Editing the website

For the manager and board members. Everything is done in the editing dashboard at **https://the-moors.sanity.studio** (also linked as "Site editors sign in" at the bottom of every page). Sign in with the email address you were invited with.

Changes appear on the website **about one minute** after you press **Publish**.

## Post news

1. Click **News**, then the pencil icon (Create).
2. Write the **Headline** in English. Add the Spanish headline below it if you have one.
3. Under **Web address**, click **Generate**.
4. Pick the **Date** and one or more **Categories** (President's Corner, From the Manager, Hotwire, Security, Associations, Community).
5. Write a **Short summary**. It shows on the news cards and the home page.
6. Add a **Photo** if you have one.
7. Write the **Article**. Use the toolbar for headings, bold, lists and links.
8. Click **Publish**.

The newest three posts appear on the home page automatically.

## Add an event

1. Click **Events**, then Create.
2. Fill in the **Event name**, click **Generate** for the web address, and choose when it **Starts** (and **Ends**, if you know).
3. For an all-day event, such as a flea market, switch on **All-day event**.
4. For a standing meeting with no single date (for example "Third Tuesday of every month"), leave **Starts** empty and fill in **Repeats** instead.
5. Fill in the **Place**, a **Short summary**, and any **Details**.
6. Click **Publish**.

Events sort themselves. Once an event is over, it moves to "Past events" the next morning.

## Upload a document

1. Click **Documents**, then Create.
2. Give it a **Name** and a one-line **Description**, and choose its **Type**.
3. Drag the PDF into **Upload the file**.
4. Click **Publish**. It appears on the Residents → Documents page.

## Show an alert across the site

For gate closures, storms, water shut-offs and similar:

1. Open **Site settings**, then the **Alert banner** tab.
2. Switch on **Show the banner**, choose **Notice** or **Urgent**, and write the message.
3. Publish. To remove it, switch the banner off and publish again.

## Change office hours, phone numbers or the payment link

**Site settings**, then the **Office and security** or **Links** tab.

## Spanish

Every text field has an **English** and an **Español** box. If you leave Español empty, the Spanish site shows the English text with a short note that a translation is coming. You never have to translate before you publish.

## Edit a page

Pages (Rules, Services, Security and so on) are under **Pages**. Edit the text and publish. Only administrators can change a page's web address, because changing it breaks links in emails and flyers.

## Undo a mistake

Every document keeps its history. Open the document, click the clock icon at the top right, pick an earlier version and click **Restore**.
