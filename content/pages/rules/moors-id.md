<!-- en -->
| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Cost** | $6, by check or money order |
| **Resident ID** | Expires every 3 years |
| **Renter ID** | Expires at the end of the lease |
| **Guest IDs** | Arranged through the management office |

Bring your Moors ID whenever you use the Club Center. Security patrols may ask anyone walking in the common areas to show a Moors ID.
