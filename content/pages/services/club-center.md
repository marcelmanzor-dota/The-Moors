The Moors Club Center is open to residents and their guests. **Bring your Moors ID** to use the facilities.

- Pets are not allowed inside the Club Center.
- Owners are responsible for the conduct of their family and guests.
- Club Center staff: [recstaff@themoors.com](mailto:recstaff@themoors.com)

## Programs and events

- [Tennis program](/services/tennis-program/)
- Flea market and food truck events. See the [calendar](/events/).
